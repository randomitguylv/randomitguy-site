## Original Pulitzer Prize-Winning Content (With Slight Modifications)

I was recently held hostage by my Sharp Aquos LC-60LE632U and firmware update 311U1204061. When I attempted to run the Netflix app, I was told I couldn’t proceed unless I updated the firmware. After 2 attempts to update the firmware via the network, I turned to Google and found that this was a common problem. You’ll need to update the firmware manually via a USB thumb drive to resolve the issue. See the following instructions:

1. Go to the Sharp USA [product download site](http://www.sharpusa.com/Support.aspx "http://www.sharpusa.com/Support.aspx")
